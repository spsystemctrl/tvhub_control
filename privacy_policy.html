TVHub – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

TVHub ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by PSBM Labs. TVHub is a media player for watching live TV and sports streams, movies and other videos, and we designed it so that your personal data stays on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains what the app accesses, what it stores, what it never collects, and the choices available to you. By installing or using TVHub, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Our Core Privacy Principle

TVHub does not require an account, a login or a sign-up. We do not operate a server that stores your personal content. The playlists, links, favorites, watch history, resume positions and settings that you create inside the app are saved only in the app's private local storage (a Hive database) on your device. We cannot see them, and they are never uploaded to us.

Information leaves your device only in these cases: when you play a stream or video from a server you chose, when advertisements are loaded through Google AdMob, when you send a message through the Contact Us form, and when the app downloads small configuration files from our hosting. Each case is explained below.

3. Information Processed by Each Feature

  • Video Player and Streaming – When you play a stream or video, your device connects directly to the server that hosts it. That server is decided by the playlist or link you use, not by us. The operator of that server can see your IP address, basic device and app information and what you are watching, under its own privacy practices. We do not proxy, record or receive this traffic.
  • Playlists and Links – The names and links that you add, and the channel or video lists loaded from them, are processed on your device and stored locally. If a link contains an access token, username or password, it is stored as part of that link and is sent only to the server it points to. Please share your playlist links carefully.
  • Favorites, Watch History and Resume – The items you mark as favorite, the videos you have watched and the position where you stopped are saved only on your device. You can remove them from inside the app at any time.
  • Local Video Files – If you choose a video file from your device, it is played on your device. The app reads only the file you choose, and the file is never uploaded.
  • Player, Theme and Font Settings – Your preferences, such as playback speed, video fit, theme and font, are saved locally on your device.
  • App Configuration and Updates – When the app starts or reconnects to the internet, it downloads small configuration files, such as advertisement settings and update information, from our hosting on GitHub Pages, and it may load the Terms and Privacy pages from there. Like any web request, this lets the host see your IP address and basic request information. These requests do not contain your playlists, history or other personal content.
  • Connectivity Check – To know whether you are online, the app checks your device's network status and may make a lightweight connection test to public internet servers.

4. Permissions We Request and Why

  • Internet and Network state – to load streams and videos, advertisements and configuration files, to check connectivity, and to send a message if you use the Contact Us form.
  • Advertising ID – used by Google AdMob to show and measure advertisements. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Wake lock – to keep your device awake while a video is playing.
  • Photos, videos or file access (optional) – only to let you pick a video file from your device, using the Android file picker or media permission available on your Android version. If you do not play local files, you can deny it.

We do not access your contacts, SMS messages, call logs, microphone, camera or location. Advertisement partners may work out a general region from your IP address, as described in Section 6.

5. Contact Form

If you voluntarily use the Contact Us screen, the name, email address, subject and message you type are sent through the EmailJS delivery service so that our support team can reply to you. This information is used only to answer your query and is not used for marketing or sold to anyone. If you prefer not to share these details, simply do not use the form and email us directly instead.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

TVHub is free and is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded formats. To show ads, Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device information such as model, operating system version, language and general region
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's own privacy practices: policies.google.com/privacy

You can review or withdraw your ad personalization consent at any time using the "Manage Ad Privacy Choices" button on this screen.

The app also uses Google Play In-App Review to optionally ask for a rating, and Google Fonts to load typefaces.

7. Third-Party Streams and Content Providers

The streams, channels, movies and videos you play with TVHub are provided by third parties that we do not own or control. When you open their content, their servers, players and websites may collect information about you, such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for their practices, so please review them if you have concerns, and play content only from sources you trust.

8. Your Consent Choices (GDPR / UK)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown when you first open the app, in line with GDPR and UK data protection requirements. Your choice can be changed at any time from this screen.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your content is stored only on your device, you remain in full control of it. You may delete any playlist, favorite or history entry from inside the app. Uninstalling TVHub removes the entire local database and all saved settings from your device permanently. We hold no backup copy, so deleted data cannot be recovered by us or by you. Data held by third parties, such as advertising partners and stream providers, is handled under their own policies.

10. Children's Privacy

TVHub is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age applicable in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Content from third-party sources may not be suitable for children, so parents and guardians should supervise its use. If you believe a child has provided us information through the contact form, please write to us and we will delete it.

11. Data Security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage area, which other applications cannot read on a non-rooted device. Our requests to our own hosting use an encrypted HTTPS connection. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure, and some third-party streams may be delivered without encryption. We recommend keeping a screen lock enabled on your device.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data held about you. Almost all TVHub data is stored only on your device, so these rights are exercised directly there. For data collected by our advertising partner, please use the Google link in Section 6. You can also write to us at the email address in Section 14.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to opt out of its sale or sharing for advertising purposes. TVHub does not sell personal data; requests about advertising data should be directed to Google using the link in Section 6.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any change will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
